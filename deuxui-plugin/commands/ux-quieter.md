Target: $ARGUMENTS

Invoke the `deuxui` skill and follow `references/ops/quieter.md`.

Quieting is reducing competition (UX-008), not removing content. Demote, do not
delete: a secondary action becomes a text button before it becomes invisible.

Four things must not get quieter — the primary action, an error or a
pre-commitment disclosure, a focus indicator, and any state. `S-FOCUS-OUTLINE`
exists because the focus ring is the most common casualty of exactly this pass,
and `R-CONTRAST` is where quieter text most often crosses 4.5:1.
