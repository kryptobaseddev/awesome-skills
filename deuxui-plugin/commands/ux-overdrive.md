Target: $ARGUMENTS

Invoke the `deuxui` skill and follow `references/ops/overdrive.md`.

Check the visitor mode before you start. On a **persuade** or **experience**
surface — a launch page, a campaign, one hero moment — this is the job. On an
**operate** or **read** surface it almost never is, and the request usually means
something else: the hierarchy is unclear, or the primary action is hard to find.
Say so and fix that instead.

The range is the contract's range. Use the top of the declared type ladder, the
declared roles, the declared depth metaphor — `S-CONTRACT-COLOR`,
`S-CONTRACT-FAMILY` and `S-CRAFT-HERO-SCALE` are what tell you whether you pushed
the system or left it.

Nothing here is licence to drop the floor. `R-CONTRAST`, `R-FOCUS-WALK`,
`R-TARGET` and `S-MOTION-REDUCE` still have to pass, and a hero that fails contrast
is not bold, it is unreadable.
