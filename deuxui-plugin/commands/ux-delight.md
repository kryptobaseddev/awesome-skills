Target: $ARGUMENTS

Invoke the `deuxui` skill and follow `references/ops/delight.md`.

Find a moment that already carries feeling and stop looking anywhere else: effort
worth acknowledging, a wait that could be informative rather than blank
(NUM-014), a first-run or empty state with nothing in it yet (`R-STATE-EMPTY`).
A celebration manufactured for an ordinary click is worse than neutral clarity.

Hard limits, and they are detectors rather than preferences: no emoji standing in
for an icon or a heading (`S-SLOP-EMOJI`), nothing that only moves
(`S-MOTION-REDUCE` — the character has to survive reduced motion), nothing that
pressures or misleads to raise a number (`S-DARK-PATTERN`), and no premature
success state (`S-STATE-PREMATURE`).

Voice is part of this and it is checked: `S-CRAFT-VOICE` reads the copy you write.
