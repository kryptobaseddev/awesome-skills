description: Make the screen say what it is — comprehension, not appearance.
argument-hint: "<the screen or flow>"

Target: $ARGUMENTS

Invoke the `deuxui` skill and follow `references/ops/clarify.md`.

Read the screen cold and answer five questions in order: where am I, what is
this, what do I do, what happened, and what went wrong and what now. Each "no" is
a defect with a rule behind it.

Fix the copy before touching the layout — copy is the cheapest intervention and
usually the right one. Label actions with their outcome, keep the accessible name
containing the visible label, and drop any term that only exists in the database.
