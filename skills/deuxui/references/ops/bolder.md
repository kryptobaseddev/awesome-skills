# bolder — raise one part to the conviction the rest already implies

An amplification request, almost always scoped to something that already exists.
The surrounding page and its system are the given.

Derived from impeccable's `bolder` (Apache-2.0) — see NOTICE.md.

## Scope is sovereign

"Everything else stays" is a literal instruction. Touch only the named target. Do
not restyle its neighbours, do not migrate the page to a new idea, and do not add
a colour, face, radius, shadow or primitive the surface does not already own.

If the existing system genuinely cannot express the direction, **stop and ask**,
naming the exact addition and the job it would do. Widening the system on your
own initiative is CTX-005, and it is the failure this operation invites.

## Reject the reflex first

The reflex answer — more effects, bigger shadow, a gradient — is the opposite of
bold. It is also what every detector in the craft floor is built to catch:
`S-SLOP-GRADIENT`, `S-CRAFT-HALO`, `S-SLOP-BLUR`, `S-CRAFT-HARD-SHADOW`. If your
plan for "bolder" trips those, the plan is louder, not bolder.

## Why it reads flat

A flat section is usually one that has quietly opted out of the system's own
strongest moves. Look at what its neighbours do that it does not: display type at
full strength, the structural device that carries meaning, the signature motif,
the density and the pacing. The most reliable bolder pass brings the target up to
the level its neighbours already reach, **in the system's own vocabulary**.

- Amplify what the system owns. The bolder version should look more like this
  brand, not less.
- Keep the content true. Existing claims are in scope: preserve them unless the
  user supplies replacements. If real evidence is essential and absent, ask.
- Commit, then quiet everything around the move so it is legible. If every
  element got louder, the section got flatter.
- Give it its own rhythm — a shift in density or pace, so it reads as a peak in
  the scroll rather than more of the same.

## The skeleton test

Strip the copy out and look at the bare structure. Does it still say what this
section is and why it matters, through hierarchy and the system's devices alone?
If it only works once the words return, the boldness is in the text size.

## Verify

| Detector | What it settles |
|---|---|
| `S-CONTRACT-COLOR`, `S-CONTRACT-FAMILY`, `S-CONTRACT-RADIUS` | Nothing new entered the system. |
| `S-CRAFT-HERO-SCALE` | Emphasis did not become shouting. |
| `S-SLOP-GRADIENT`, `S-CRAFT-HALO`, `S-CRAFT-HARD-SHADOW` | The reflex answer was rejected. |
| `R-BASELINE-DIFF` | Everything outside the named target is unchanged. |
