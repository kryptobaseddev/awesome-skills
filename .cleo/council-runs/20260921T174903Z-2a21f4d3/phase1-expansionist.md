### Advisor: Expansionist

**Frame:** Forget the constraints. What's the biggest version of this? What opportunity is sitting right in front of us that nobody is talking about?

**Evidence anchored:**

- `references/rules/registry.yaml:2543-2930` (evidence item 1) — the 20 laws are **not prose**. Every `LAW-nn` carries a structured `principle` / `limits` / `apply_when` / `MUST` / `MUST_NOT` / **`verify:`** block. That is ~40 pre-authored, pre-sourced *verification predicates*, already written, already limit-bounded, already committed to git — and consumed by zero lines of code (`grep -c LAW scripts/ux_report.py` = 0). Several are not even new work: `LAW-02.verify: "Target bounds pass Section 06"` is `targets.js`, already shipping. `LAW-04.verify: "Visual groups match semantic groups at narrow and wide layouts"` is `layout.js` + `ab snapshot`, already run at all five viewports. `LAW-12.verify: "The task hierarchy survives real content and small viewports"` is the reflow matrix that already executes every run. The dormant asset is not a design-knowledge gap; it is a **missing index from `LAW-nn.verify` to detector IDs that already fire.**

- `scripts/ux_browser.sh:102,151` + `references/rules/detectors.yaml:176-178` + `scripts/ux_report.py:302` — the driver writes `$OUT/screens/${R}_${vp}.png` for **every route × every viewport**, plus `${R}_state_{abort,empty,offline}.png`, on every single run. `ux_report.py` contains zero reads of `screens/` or `*.png`. `R-BASELINE-DIFF` is a declared `engine: visual` detector whose only appearance in the reporter is a hardcoded NOT_RUN string. The installed `agent-browser` supports `diff screenshot --baseline` **and** `diff snapshot --baseline` (verified: `agent-browser diff --help`), and `references/preserve.md:74-81` already tells a *human* to run it by hand. A rendered-pixel corpus is being generated and thrown away every run.

- `references/workflows/init.md:51` declares `DESIGN.md → tokens, component inventory, stack, density, themes, motion`; `grep -rn "DESIGN" scripts/` returns **nothing**. `uxconfig.py` is "the only reader of `.deluxui/ux.config.yaml`" — so the project's *visual system* is the one layer of the three-layer model with no machine reader. Meanwhile `references/preserve.md:33-43` already demands the identity be stated in **observable values** ("use hex, OKLCH or token names, not moods — *'Modern' is not a colour*"). The hard conceptual work of making design declarable is done and sitting in a file nothing parses.

- Evidence item 6 (A/B record) — knowledge-shaped assertions **17/18 vs 17/18**; verifiability-shaped **15/18 vs 1/18**. Read as an opportunity rather than a constraint: there is a measured **15x** lane and a measured **1.0x** lane, and every latent asset above sits in the 15x one.

- Evidence item 7 — `engine: visual` has **2 of 166** detectors; HEURISTIC is **16 of 190**. Both are the smallest buckets in the architecture, and both are exactly where craft would have to live. The scarcity is the opportunity: the visual engine is a stub on top of infrastructure that is already built.

**Findings (opportunities, from my frame only):**

1. **Activate the laws as the design-verdict layer — they are already predicates, not opinions.** The skill's own constitution shipped 20 laws with machine-shaped `verify:` clauses and then wired them to nothing. Captured, this gives deluxui something no design skill has: *design reasoning that reports PASS / FAIL / NOT_RUN per named law with a citable basis*. Three tiers of landing zone already exist and need no new engine — laws whose `verify:` maps onto a live probe (LAW-02→`targets.js`, LAW-04/12→`layout.js`+a11ytree across the viewport matrix, LAW-13→a computed-token equivalence walk over the DOM traversal `contrast.js` already performs) become **live detectors**; the rest land on the `manual` attestation bus, which today collapses **11 distinct judgement rules into the single `M-DESIGN-REVIEW` slot** (`detectors.yaml:294-296`) and could carry one law-indexed evidence line each. Asymmetry: the expensive half — sourcing, limits, MUST/MUST_NOT, the verify clauses themselves, the attestation file format, the NOT_RUN gate — is **already paid for and in git**; the missing half is a mapping table. A law-indexed verdict is permanent category-defining surface for the cost of a YAML index.

2. **Compile `DESIGN.md` into a contract, and deluxui stops being a critic of design systems and becomes the *prover* of one.** Today craft cannot enter the architecture legitimately because taste has no external basis — evidence item 7 says the whole machine is built to adjudicate things with a defensible source, and only 16 of 190 rules are taste-adjacent. A machine-readable DESIGN.md dissolves that: once the project declares its own colour ramp steps, type scale, spacing scale and elevation set, every generative choice becomes a **conformance question against a basis the project itself authored** — STANDARD-shaped, not HEURISTIC-shaped. "This card uses a shadow level that is not in your elevation set" is as checkable as a contrast ratio and needs no opinion at all. The four axes evidence item 3 found completely absent (colour ramp, focal point/visual weight, grid, elevation) are precisely the four that are *declarable*. This is the category-redefining framing: the question is not "director or auditor" but **compiler**. And it is the one thing impeccable's 6,576 prose lines (item 5) structurally cannot do at any volume — prose cannot adjudicate conformance to a system it has never read. Asymmetry: one schema plus one reader alongside `uxconfig.py`, against permanent ownership of the only category where design craft is falsifiable.

3. **Harvest the screenshot corpus the runtime tier already throws away — the visual engine is 2 detectors on top of a full measurement rig.** Every run already produces N routes × 5 viewports × 3 forced states of rendered pixels, and nothing reads them. The immediate win is free: the driver screenshots but never calls the `diff screenshot --baseline` / `diff snapshot --baseline` that the installed CLI provides, so `R-BASELINE-DIFF` and the `M-PRESERVE-REGRESSION` attestation are structurally unreachable — two lines in `ux_browser.sh` and the preserve ladder's central claim ("being wrong about departing rewrites someone's product") becomes **measured rather than argued**, which is the strongest thing in `preserve.md` and currently the least verified. The larger prize is that pixels are where the remaining craft axes are *empirically* checkable and nowhere else: focal point and visual weight are luminance-and-area distribution over a render, colour-ramp coherence is a perceptual-distance measurement over actually-painted colours, optical alignment is a pixel fact. Asymmetry: the corpus is a **sunk cost already being paid every run**; the engine slot, the `visual` label, the confidence field and the NOT_RUN contract all exist. This also compounds — the corpus is longitudinal by construction, so the same exhaust that grades today's change is a design-regression history tomorrow.

**Verdict from this lens:** The plan is far too small, and it is small in a specific and correctable way — it is framed as *"should we add design prose?"*, which is the only version of this that the evidence already shows to be worth nothing (item 6: 17/18 vs 17/18, and design prose is knowledge-shaped by construction). The biggest version does not add a single page of design knowledge. It activates three capabilities the skill already owns and already paid for — 40 committed `verify:` predicates, an unread `DESIGN.md`, and a rendered-pixel corpus generated and discarded on every run — and lands design craft in the lane where the same A/B measured a 15x delta.

**Single sharpest point:** deluxui is the only design skill in existence that already has a verdict gate, a citable-basis registry, a rendered-pixel measurement rig and 40 pre-written verification predicates sitting inert in its own constitution — activating them makes it the first skill that can *prove* design craft rather than assert it, which is a category no amount of competing prose can enter.
